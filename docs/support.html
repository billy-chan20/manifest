Support

Support Overview

For assistance with this BSP, use one of the following support channels.

Intel Representative

For commercial engagements, roadmap discussions, platform planning, access requests, or ecosystem enablement, contact your Intel representative through your existing account or program channels.

Note: Your Intel representative is the primary point of contact for support guidance, issue escalation, and engagement with the appropriate Intel teams.

Intel Premier Support

For BSP, BIOS/IFWI, platform enablement, or other technical issues, submit a case through Intel Premier Support.

Intel Premier Support

Note: When submitting a case, include relevant platform information, software versions, reproduction steps, and logs to accelerate issue triage and resolution.

Resource & Documentation Center (RDC)

For technical collateral, reference documentation, datasheets, and platform resources, visit the Intel Resource & Documentation Center (RDC).

Resource & Documentation Center

Note: Access to certain materials may require additional permissions. Contact your Intel representative if you require access.

Support Scope

This repository provides a reference Android Base BSP and related documentation. Product-specific integrations, Android OS customization, deployment services, long-term maintenance, and commercial support are provided by your ISV or OSV partner.

Intel provides the reference BSP foundation and associated documentation. Responsibilities beyond the reference BSP, including product integration, solution deployment, operating system customization, and application-level solutions, reside with the respective ISV or OSV.
